Function

Rigid work helmet: the diver breathes air supplied from the surface and talks to the support team.

Use

Jobs beyond 12 m and long dives: port construction, welding, demolition.

Features

  • Surface supply through the umbilical: air does not run out like a cylinder.
  • Demand regulator plus an adjustable steady flow for ventilation and defogging.
  • Emergency valve connected to a bailout cylinder on the diver's back.
  • Built-in microphone and speakers for communication with the surface.
  • Reinforced fibreglass shell that protects the head from impacts.

Function

Wired link between diver and surface: real-time voice throughout the job.

Use

Essential when the diver guides the crane operator from the bottom while placing blocks.

Features

  • Cable inside the umbilical: no radio waves, clear voice even in murky water.
  • Two-wire mode with push-to-talk or four-wire mode with an always-open line.
  • One surface station handles one or two divers, who can also talk to each other.
  • Output to record the dive communications.

Function

Small underwater robot controlled from the surface, with camera and lights.

Use

Inspection of submerged pipelines, inside them too, and of areas too deep or narrow for a diver.

Features

  • Tether carrying commands and live video to the surface monitor.
  • Own lights to see where sunlight does not reach.
  • The footage remains as documentation for the client.

Function

Surface station that manages the diver's air and safety during the dive.

Use

Sites where we work with surface-supplied helmets.

Features

  • Air distribution panel to the umbilical, with an emergency reserve.
  • The umbilical combines air hose, phone cable and a hose to read depth from the surface.
  • A surface operator follows the diver the whole time.
  • Transportable container: it goes wherever the site is.

Function

They produce breathing air for cylinders and for surface supply.

Use

At our base and, with the portable models, right on site.

Features

  • Coltri is an Italian manufacturer of breathing-air compressors.
  • The air goes through filters that remove moisture, oil and impurities.
  • In Europe, divers' breathing air must meet standard EN 12021.

Function

"Wet" welding: the diver welds directly in the water, without dry chambers.

Use

Launching ramps, cradles, steel structures, repairs and fixing of anodes.

Features

  • Welding generator at the surface and an insulated electrode holder for use in water.
  • Waterproofed electrodes made for underwater welding.
  • Current reaches the diver only when requested, through a safety switch at the surface.

Function

Torch for cutting metal underwater with exothermic rods fed by oxygen.

Use

Demolition of iron structures, removal of wrecks and damaged metal parts.

Features

  • The rod is ignited by an electric pulse and keeps burning as long as it gets oxygen.
  • Temperatures above 5,000 °C: it cuts thick steel and iron.
  • Rods can be bent to reach awkward spots.

Function

Underwater pneumatic hammer powered by compressed air.

Use

Demolition of concrete and rock underwater, seabed preparation.

Features

  • Air comes from the surface compressor through a hose.
  • It works where electric tools cannot be used.

Function

High-pressure cleaning with hot water, and with sand for the hardest surfaces.

Use

Cleaning of hulls and structures, removal of growth and rust before inspections or welding.

Features

  • Hot water dissolves dirt and greasy residues better.
  • Wet sandblasting takes metal back to bare, ready for checks and work.

Function

They suck up sand, mud and debris from the seabed.

Use

Underwater digging, cleaning of seabeds and foundations before laying blocks.

Features

  • The airlift works with air: air injected at the bottom of the pipe rises and drags water and debris with it.
  • The material is discharged away from the work area.
